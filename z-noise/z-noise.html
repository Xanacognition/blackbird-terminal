WONDERBLUE-OS

Tracker firmware for the FM-1: chopped breaks with Akai-style punch-ins, genre-driven songs with generative FM bass and keys over the chords you pick, and a sit-back mode that plays itself.

START

Power on, then turn the GENRE knob and press NEW SONG twice. Each press rolls a new song: tempo, key, chords, drums and sounds. PLAY runs the pattern chain. SIT starts playback and leaves the machine to itself: it moves between sections, drops the drums out, mutates parts and lets the FM timbres drift.

Tap a track name on the screen to select it. The pads, knobs and pages all follow the selected track.

WRITE

  • Tap a row to put the cursor there. Tap it again to add or remove a note.
  • TRK page knobs edit note, velocity, effect and effect amount.
  • Turn on REC and play pads while it runs to punch in on the nearest row. While stopped, each pad writes a row and steps down.
  • RPT is note repeat: hold a pad to roll it in time.
  • SNG keeps your songs. Signed in, they follow you to every device with this page; BACKUP saves them all to one file you can import anywhere.
  • CHD picks the chord progression (Axis pop, 50s, jazz, Andalusian and more). Bass, melody and pads move to the new chords and keep their rhythm. Pads 9–16 are the chord slots; tap one, then a chord on pads 1–7.

LOAD WAV on the MST page drops your own break onto the selected break track, cut into 16 slices.

Browser build of the Wonderblue-OS firmware concept. Every sound is synthesized live or rendered at power-on: the breaks are built from FM and noise drum voices and then crushed to 12-bit, 26 kHz. The song you are working on autosaves in this browser; saved songs live in the SNG library.
