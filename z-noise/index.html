START

Power on, then pick a vibe above. PLAY runs the pattern chain. SIT starts playback and leaves the machine to itself: it moves between sections, drops the drums out, mutates parts and lets the FM timbres drift.

Tap a track name on the screen to select it. The pads, knobs and pages all follow the selected track.

WRITE

  • Tap a row to put the cursor there. Tap it again to add or remove a note.
  • TRK page knobs edit note, velocity, effect and effect amount.
  • Turn on REC and play pads while it runs to punch in on the nearest row. While stopped, each pad writes a row and steps down.
  • RPT is note repeat: hold a pad to roll it in time.

EFFECT COLUMN

G nplay with a chance of (n+1)/16

Bplay the slice backwards

LOAD WAV on the MST page drops your own break onto the selected break track, cut into 16 slices.

Browser build of the Z-NOISE firmware concept. Every sound is synthesized live or rendered at power-on: the breaks are built from FM and noise drum voices and then crushed to 12-bit, 26 kHz. Your work autosaves in this browser.
